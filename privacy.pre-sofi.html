Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

This policy explains how Integral Body Institute ("we") processes personal data in FasciApp, in line with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Controller

Integral Body Institute, contact contact@integralbodyinstitute.com.

2. What we collect

  • Account: email address and a securely hashed password.
  • Practitioner profile: name, contact details (phone, WhatsApp availability, email, website), up to 3 work locations (city, country, optional address and label, map coordinates), profile photo, practice tags and short bio.
  • Session log: client initials, date, duration and an optional comment. Only initials are stored, never client names.

3. Why and on what basis

To run the directory and the session log (performance of our agreement with you, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Your profile is shown publicly only if you tick "Show me publicly on the map" (consent, Art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time.

4. Where the data is stored

Data is stored with Supabase in the EU (Frankfurt, Germany). Addresses are converted into map coordinates with OpenStreetMap Nominatim. Map tiles are loaded from Esri. We do not sell your data.

5. Who can see it

Public profiles are visible to anyone using FasciApp. Private profiles and session logs are visible only to you (protected by row-level security).

6. How long we keep it

Until you delete your profile or account. Deleting your account removes your profile and session logs.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, export and delete your data, to restrict or object to processing, and to withdraw consent. To delete your data or use any of these rights, email contact@integralbodyinstitute.com. You can also complain to the Estonian Data Protection Inspectorate (Andmekaitse Inspektsioon) or your local authority.
