Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

This policy explains how personal data is processed in FasciApp (fasciapp.app) under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Controller

Body Awareness Center OÜ, Telliskivi 49-39, 10611 Tallinn, Estonia (Registration No. 14148717). For contact details, see the Contact section below. We have not appointed a data protection officer.

2. What we process, why, on what basis and for how long

  • Account (email, password stored hashed by Supabase, sign-in times): to give you an account and run the service. Basis: contract, Art. 6(1)(b). Kept until your account is deleted.
  • Practitioner listing (name, optional practice / business name, phone, WhatsApp availability, email, website, up to 3 locations with address and map coordinates, photo, training tags, bio): to show you in the directory. Basis: contract, Art. 6(1)(b); it is public only when you tick "Show me publicly on the map", and you can untick it at any time. When public, your listing can be seen by anyone, but your email and phone (and WhatsApp contact) are visible only to signed-in users; visitors who are not signed in never receive them. Kept until you delete the listing or your account.
  • Messages (sender, recipient, text up to 1,000 characters, sent and read times): to deliver private messages between signed-in users. Basis: contract, Art. 6(1)(b). Only sender and recipient can read them; BAI reads a message only when handling a report about it or where required by law (legitimate interest in safety, Art. 6(1)(f)). Private messages are kept for 12 months and then deleted automatically, and are also deleted when the account is deleted.
  • Session log (client initials, date, duration, optional comment): stored in our database (Supabase, EU) so you can use it on any device. For this content you are the controller and BAI is your processor (see Terms §16). Do not enter names, health details or identifying notes. Kept until you delete the entry or your account. PDF and email exports are created on your device and sent from your own email app; BAI does not receive them.
  • News emails (your email, and when you opted in or out): FasciApp news is sent by Body Awareness Center OÜ via its newsletter provider Flodesk only to people who opted in by ticking "Send me FasciApp news". Basis: consent, Art. 6(1)(a). Every news email contains an unsubscribe link. You can withdraw anytime with that link or by unticking the box in your profile. Kept until you withdraw consent or delete your account.
  • Usage statistics. To understand how FasciApp is used and to improve it, we collect anonymous usage statistics: which screens and features are opened, the time of use, and the device type, browser and operating system. Each visit gets a random identifier that exists only while the app is open and is not saved on your device. We do not collect your name, email address or IP address, and we do not track you across other websites. We store these statistics in our own database (Supabase). Legal basis: our legitimate interest in keeping the app working well and improving it (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). You can object at any time by emailing info@bodyawareness.ee. Retention: usage statistics are deleted after 12 months.
  • Reports and support emails: to handle them. Basis: legal obligation (EU Digital Services Act, Art. 6(1)(c)) and legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)). Kept up to 2 years after the case closes.
  • Technical data (IP address, browser, time of request) in server logs of our providers: to deliver and secure the service. Basis: legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f). Kept for the providers' short log periods.

Providing data is voluntary, but without an email and password you cannot create an account. We make no automated decisions or profiling with legal or similarly significant effects. We do not sell your data and use no advertising tools.

3. Processors and other recipients

  • Supabase Inc. (database, sign-in, messaging) – data stored in the EU (AWS eu-west-3, Paris, France), under a data processing agreement. Supabase is a US company; any access from outside the EEA is covered by the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.
  • GitHub Inc. (hosting of the app via GitHub Pages, USA) – receives your IP address and request data when the app loads. Transfers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.
  • Flodesk Inc. (newsletter emails, USA) – receives the email address and name of people who opted in to FasciApp news. Transfers rely on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.
  • ipapi.co (Kloudend Inc., USA) and GeoJS (get.geojs.io) – IP location services that can receive your IP address to estimate your approximate location. Transfers rely on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.
  • Photon (komoot, Germany, using OpenStreetMap data) – address search; receives the address you type and your IP address.
  • OpenStreetMap Foundation (Nominatim address search, UK) – receives the address you type and your IP when you place a location on the map. The UK has an EU adequacy decision.
  • Esri (map tiles, server.arcgisonline.com, USA) – receives your IP address and the map area you view.
  • Content delivery networks loading the app's code and fonts: Google Fonts (Google, USA), unpkg and cdnjs (Cloudflare, USA) and jsDelivr – they receive your IP address. Transfers rely on the Data Privacy Framework or Standard Contractual Clauses.
  • YouTube (Google, USA), Spotify (Sweden) and SoundCloud (Germany) – videos, podcasts and audio embedded in the Fascia Lab. They receive your IP address and may set their own cookies only when you open or play an embedded item. Transfers to the USA rely on the Data Privacy Framework.
  • WhatsApp (Meta, Ireland/USA) – if you tap a WhatsApp contact button, you leave FasciApp and WhatsApp processes the chat under its own privacy policy.
  • Authorities, courts or advisers where required by law or to defend legal claims.

4. Cookies and device storage

FasciApp sets no tracking or advertising cookies. It stores in your browser's local storage your sign-in session and a cache of the app's files so it works as an installed app (strictly necessary). Usage statistics (section 2) use no cookies and store nothing on your device: their random visit identifier exists only in memory while the app is open. You can clear all of it in your browser settings at any time (you will be signed out).

5. Minors

FasciApp accounts are for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly process data of children; if you believe a child has an account, tell us and we will delete it.

6. Your rights

You can ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction, data portability, and object to processing based on legitimate interest. You can untick public visibility, edit or delete your listing and session entries in the app at any time. To delete your whole account (including messages and session log) or use any right, contact us (see the Contact section); we reply within one month. You may complain to the Estonian Data Protection Inspectorate (Andmekaitse Inspektsioon), Tatari 39, 10134 Tallinn, Estonia (aki.ee), or to the authority in your country.

7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit, and database rules (row-level security) ensure that private listings, messages and session logs can be read only by the users they belong to.

8. Changes

We will update this policy when our processing changes and tell account holders about material changes.

9. Contact

Body Awareness Center OÜ, Telliskivi 49-39, 10611 Tallinn, Estonia (Registration No. 14148717). Email for questions, privacy and GDPR requests, and confidential conduct reports: info@bodyawareness.ee
